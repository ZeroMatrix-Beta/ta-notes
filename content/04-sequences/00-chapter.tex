\chapter{Sequences and Convergence}
\label{ch:sequences}

% Transition: Claude Opus 5 (Medium)
Open and closed sets describe a space statically: which points sit comfortably inside a set, and
which sit on its edge. Sequences make the same information dynamic, and in a metric space they
turn out to characterise both notions. This chapter defines convergence and accumulation points,
proves that limits are unique, and shows how openness and closedness can be tested with sequences
alone --- the tool that almost every argument in the following chapters uses.
