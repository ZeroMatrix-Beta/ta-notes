\chapter{Change of Variables, Fubini and Feynman's Trick}
\label{ch:change_of_variables}

% Transition: Claude Opus 5 (Medium)
With the integral defined, this chapter collects the three tools that make it computable: the
change of variables formula, which replaces an awkward domain by a convenient one at the cost of
a Jacobian factor; Fubini's theorem, which reduces a multiple integral to iterated
one-dimensional ones; and differentiation under the integral sign, the trick that evaluates
integrals having no elementary antiderivative by turning them into an ODE in a parameter.
