\chapter{Metric, Normed and Inner-Product Spaces}
\label{ch:metric_spaces}

% Transition: Claude Opus 5 (Medium)
This chapter abstracts the notion of distance from $\mathbb{R}^n$ to an arbitrary set. It sets up
the hierarchy of structured spaces --- topological, metric, normed, inner-product --- that the
rest of the course rests on, establishes the metric axioms and the three standard metrics $d_1$,
$d_2$, $d_3$, and closes with the geometry that an inner product buys over a mere norm: angles,
orthogonal projection, and the Cauchy--Schwarz inequality.

Corsin taught the two halves a week apart, opening the later one with a recap of the definitions
given here; \cref{sec:inner_products_recap} keeps that recap, now as a pointer back to
\cref{sec:structured_spaces} rather than a repetition.
