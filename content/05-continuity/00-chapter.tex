\chapter{Continuity}
\label{ch:continuity}

% Transition: Claude Opus 5 (Medium)
With distance, shape and convergence in place, we can finally ask the question the whole
apparatus was built for: what does it mean for a map \emph{between} two metric spaces to respect
that structure? Three equivalent answers are given --- $\varepsilon$--$\delta$, sequential and
topological --- followed by the two strengthenings that matter later, uniform continuity and
Lipschitz continuity.

Lipschitz continuity is drawn from a second tutor's slides and reached Corsin's own notes only
obliquely, as a constant used for contraction mappings; \cref{sec:lipschitz_continuity} names it
properly and places it in the hierarchy.
